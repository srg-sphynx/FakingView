FakingView adds INDmoney and Groww as read-only sources for Indian market data.

### What’s new

- Connect either provider with a personal access token in Settings. **Test Connection** checks
  live quote access, and saved tokens use your device’s secure storage.
- Search NSE and BSE instruments in the Stocks picker, chart historical OHLCV in INR, and add
  instruments to a watchlist for live REST quotes.
- Use the new sources in Pine Studio, Pine symbol requests, and MCP market-data tools.
- INDmoney covers cash listings and listed NSE derivatives. Groww covers cash listings, indices,
  and listed derivatives on NSE and BSE.

### Download and verification

Download **FakingView-v0.1.0-beta.2-macos-arm64.zip** for **Apple Silicon on macOS 14 or newer**.
Use the [installation and provider setup guide](https://github.com/srg-sphynx/FakingView/blob/v0.1.0-beta.2/docs/getting-started.md#indmoney-and-groww).
This is a manual-install beta without Developer ID signing or notarization.

The corresponding GPL source is attached as **FakingView-v0.1.0-beta.2-source.tar.gz**.
Download **SHA256SUMS.txt** alongside the archives and verify them with
`shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt`.

The Rust suite passed 7,847 tests, with 131 optional tests skipped. Coverage includes instrument
routing, candle parsing and time zones, and quote parsing. Formatting and all-target, all-feature
Clippy checks are part of release validation.
Live account authentication and data entitlements still require verification with your own
INDstocks or Groww account.
