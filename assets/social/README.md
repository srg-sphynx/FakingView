These are conceptual brand illustrations, not application screenshots or market performance
charts. The README's application screenshots are separate, renderer-generated frames with
explicit sample-data labels. GitHub's social preview is configured in repository Settings →
General → Social preview; the JPEG is the upload-ready export.

The original images were created with the built-in ImageGen tool on 19 November 2026, then copied
into this directory. The JPEG is a format export of the landscape original. Palette: charcoal
`#181b22`, near-white, mint `#75d4b0` and amber `#e7b577`. Keep the product name exactly
**FakingView**. Do not add broker affiliation, fabricated application UI or investment claims.

macOS market-charting app for Indian markets. Use restrained developer-tool styling in the palette
above. Large exact title: “FakingView”; secondary exact text: “Indian markets. Native charts.”;
small exact text: “Kite Connect • Pine Script • macOS”. Strong clean typography on the left,
precise abstract candlestick forms and subtle grids on the right. An original white F and
mint/amber candles, no other brand logos. Conceptual artwork, no fake app UI, real prices, market
performance claims, brokerage logos, watermark or affiliation claims. Flat crisp shapes,
sophisticated spacing, understated texture, high readability and a GitHub-safe centered composition.
